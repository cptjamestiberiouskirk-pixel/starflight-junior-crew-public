or claim ownership of another party's work. Retain complete notices with any
authorized distribution. See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and

A general public-release license has not been selected or cleared for the

Provenance, paid generation and attribution are not treated as proof of permission.

This describes current permission status, not a claim that every included work
has the same owner or license. The maintainer can record specific permissions
and a future release policy after those checks.
